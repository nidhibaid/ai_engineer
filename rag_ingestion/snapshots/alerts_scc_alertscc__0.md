# Sign Up for Emergency Alerts

Smartphone displaying the AlertSCC logo.

## What Is AlertSCC

AlertSCC is the County of Santa Clara’s emergency alert system. It sends important messages fast to help keep people safe.

## Sign Up or Update Your Information

You can sign up for **AlertSCC** for free and without a password.

Click the **Sign Up** to create an account and enter your contact information.

 [Update Account Information](https://member.everbridge.net/453003085615038/login)

### **Need Help with AlertSCC?**

[**How to Sign Up and Update Information on AlertSCC**](https://oem.santaclaracounty.gov/sign-up-for-alerts)

If you need help signing up or updating your information, you can email: [AlertSCC.Admin@oem.sccgov.org](mailto:AlertSCC.Admin@oem.sccgov.org) .

### How You Get  
Alerts

You can get alerts by:

* Cell phone
* Landline
* Email

You choose how you want to get alerts and set up alerts for your loved ones.

### What Alerts You May Get

AlertSCC will only send alerts in an emergency for:

* Fires
* Earthquakes
* Bad weather
* Public safety

### Why AlertSCC Is Important

In an emergency, getting the **right information fast can help keep you safe** .

AlertSCC can send longer and clearer messages about the area you are in. These alerts tell you what to do so you can stay safe.

### Your Information Is Safe

Your information in AlertSCC is private and secure. The information is easy to delete and can be done at any time. Only trained 911 workers use your information. Your information is never shared with private companies.

Step-by-Step Guides

## How to Sign Up and Update Information on AlertSCC

#### How to Sign Up for AlertSCC

AlertSCC uses the Everbridge system. When you sign up, you will go to the Everbridge website to create an account.

##### **Step 1: Start Your Sign Up**

1. Go to the  page.
2. Enter your email or phone number.
3. Click the box that says, “I am not a robot.”
4. Choose how you want to get your code.
5. Enter the code.
6. Click **Next** .

##### **Step 2: Add Your Information**

1. Enter your first and last name.
2. Enter your email or phone number.
3. Check the box to agree to the Terms of Use.
4. Choose how you want to get your code.
5. Enter the code.

##### **Step 3: Subscribe to AlertSCC**

1. Click **\+Add Subscriptions.**
2. Under Add Subscriptions. Search: **County of Santa Clara:AlertSCC.**
3. Click on **County of Santa Clara:AlertSCC.**
4. Check the box to agree to the Terms of Use.
5. Click **Continue** .

##### **Step 4: Complete AlertSCC Registration**

1. Look over your information to make sure it is correct.
2. Enter Location Name. For example, Home, Work, or School.
3. Enter Address.
4. Click on **Verify This Address** .
5. Look over your address to make sure it is correct.
6. Click **This is My Location** .
7. Click **Register.**
8. Add other contact information.
9. Click **Save & Continue.**
10. Add other locations.
11. Click **Save & Continue.**
12. Select Alert Subscriptions: **Santa Clara County, Extreme Weather Alerts** .
13. Click **Save & Continue.**
14. Select your preferred language to receive alerts.
15. Click **Save & Continue.**
16. Check everything one more time.
17. Click **Finish.**

You are now signed up for AlertSCC.

**Quick Tip 💡:** Add AlertSCC numbers and email to your phone's contact list so you don't miss an alert.

* Phone number: **(408) 808‑7871**
* Text number: **88911**
* Email: **[noreply@everbridge.net](mailto:noreply@everbridge.net)**

#### How to Update your Information

##### **Step 1: Log In**

1. Click [Update Account Information](https://member.everbridge.net/453003085615038/login) .
2. Click **Get Started** .
3. Enter your email address or phone number.
4. Click the box that says, “I am not a robot.”
5. Choose how you want to get your code.
6. Enter the code to open your account.
7. Click **Next** .

##### **Step 2: Edit Your Information**

1. On the **Home** page, you will see your information.
2. Click any **blue “(Edit)” link** to change:

* Your locations
* Your phone numbers
* Your emails
* Your name
* Your subscriptions
* Your preferred language

1. When you are done, click **Save** .

##### **How to Delete Your Account**

Click **Delete My Account** on your AlertSCC profile page. Then click **OK** to finish deleting it.

Next, you will go to the Everbridge website. Click the **settings screw icon** . Choose **Delete Account** , then click the **Delete Account** button again. Finally, click **Confirm and Delete** to finish.

## More About AlertSCC

#### How Does AlertSCC Work?

AlertSCC uses the internet, cell networks, and phone lines to reach people fast.

911 workers can send alerts to:

* Cell phones
* Landlines
* Email
* TTY devices for people who are Deaf or Hard of Hearing

AlertSCC can send alerts to people in the area of the emergency. It uses your address to know whether you need an alert.

#### How do I Know if I am Signed up For AlertSCC?

Try [logging in](https://member.everbridge.net/453003085615038/login) . You can enter your email or phone number, and it will tell you if you have an account. AlertSCC doesn’t use passwords. It sends a code to your phone or email to log in.

#### Can I Get Alerts for the Places Where My Loved Ones Live?

Yes. You can add their address to your AlertSCC account to get alerts for where they live.

If they don’t live in Santa Clara County, you can sign up for alerts in their area. [Find local emergency alerts](https://www.listoscalifornia.org/alerts/)

#### Are Alerts Available in Other Languages?

**AlertSCC sends messages in English right away.** Messages in other languages might take a little longer to make. Each message has a link you can click to read it in the language you like. Text messages can only be in English or Spanish. Emails can be in more languages.

You can also look at the [Current Alerts and Disasters](https://oem.santaclaracounty.gov/current-alerts-and-disasters) page for more languages.

#### If I Give More Than One Phone Number, Which One Will be Called?

AlertSCC will try **all** your phone numbers that you entered to make sure you get the message.

#### What Happens If My Line Is Busy or I Don’t Answer?

AlertSCC will try calling again. If your voicemail picks up, AlertSCC leaves you a message in your voicemail.

#### Will AlertSCC Work With Call Blocking or Screening?

Usually, yes. Most call blocking or screening systems allow AlertSCC calls. Some phones may need you to allow the number **(408) 808‑7871** so the calls don’t get blocked.

#### Is AlertSCC Free?

AlertSCC is free. You don't pay to sign up, update account information, or delete your account.

#### How Can I Find the Most Recent Alerts?

For the most recent alerts, sign up for AlertSCC.  To see alerts that already happened, go to the [Current Alerts and Disasters](https://oem.santaclaracounty.gov/current-alerts-and-disasters) page.

#### Does AlertSCC Replace Other Alerts?

No. AlertSCC does not replace other emergency tools.  It is **another local way** to share important safety messages.

Some cities and police use **Nixle** groups inside of AlertSCC to send messages to people who sign up.

#### Do I Need to Download an App?

No. AlertSCC sends alerts by text message, phone call, landline, and/or email.

Everbridge has a [phone app](https://www.everbridge.com/platform/applications/) , if you want to get AlertSCC alerts through push notifications.

#### Does AlertSCC Send Non‑Emergency Community Messages?

No. AlertSCC only sends emergency alerts.

#### What Is Everbridge?

Everbridge is the company that runs AlertSCC. When you sign up through Everbridge, you can choose to get alerts from other cities and counties.

#### More Ways to Get Alerts

**Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEAs)** are free text messages that help keep people safe in California. You may get them for three reasons:

* **Presidential Alerts** tell everyone in the country about a big emergency.
* **Imminent Threat Alerts** warn you about dangerous weather or other serious danger.
* **AMBER Alerts** help find a missing child in your area.

Make sure your phone is set to get these alerts.

* * *

**Earthquake Warnings** can tell you an earthquake is coming before you feel shaking. You can get these warnings in three ways:

* [**MyShake App**](https://www.earthquake.ca.gov/) **:** A free app for iPhone and Android that gives sound and screen warnings in English and Spanish.
* [**Android Earthquake Alerts**](https://www.earthquake.ca.gov/get-alerts/) **:** Android phones with updates turned on get these alerts automatically.
* [**WEAs**](https://www.calalerts.org/wea.html) **:** Free emergency text messages sent to your phone.

* * *

**Wildfire Evacuation Zones**

* [**Genasys Protect**](https://genasysprotect.com/) is another way to get alerts about wildfires. It is an app that breaks Santa Clara County into smaller areas, called zones. This helps give specific, timely, and accurate information about wildfire evacuations.
