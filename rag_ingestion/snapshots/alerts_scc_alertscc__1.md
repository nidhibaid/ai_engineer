## Prepare For Extreme Weather

[](https://oem.santaclaracounty.gov/disaster-preparedness/hot-weather-safety)

### Hot Weather Safety

Extremely hot weather can result in heatstroke, heat exhaustion, heat cramps, sunburn, heat rash, and in some instances death.

[Prepare For Hot Weather](https://oem.santaclaracounty.gov/disaster-preparedness/hot-weather-safety)

[](https://oem.santaclaracounty.gov/disaster-preparedness/cold-weather-safety)

### Cold Weather Safety

Cold weather including damaging winds, rain, hail, flooding, flash flooding and winter storms can happen anytime in Santa Clara County.

[Prepare For Cold Weather](https://oem.santaclaracounty.gov/disaster-preparedness/cold-weather-safety)

## County of Santa Clara Office of Emergency Management

The County of Santa Clara Office of Emergency Management helps keep our community safe before, during, and after disasters. We work with firefighters, police, doctors, and local leaders to make emergency plans and arrange shared resources.

**Find information on:**

* AlertSCC and emergency alerts
* How to get ready for different disasters
* Help after a disaster
